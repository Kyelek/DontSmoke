## en-US — English (United States)

### Title
Quit Smoking Easy

### Short description
Log every cigarette, see how long you've gone smoke-free and stay motivated.

### Full description
Note: the app is currently available in Spanish only.

Quitting smoking is easier when you can see your progress. Quit Smoking Easy is a simple, no-fuss counter that supports you while you cut down or quit tobacco.

How does it work?
Every time you smoke, tap the cigarette. From that moment, the app counts in real time the hours, minutes and seconds you've gone without smoking. That's it: no forms, no accounts, no setup.

Messages that keep you going
Depending on how long you've been smoke-free, you get supportive messages that change with you: from the first minutes to your first day, your first week and your first smoke-free month. And if you slip, the app reminds you that one cigarette doesn't erase your progress.

Your history at a glance
• Cigarettes today and all-time total.
• History grouped by day with the time of each cigarette.
• Long-press an entry to delete it if you made a mistake.
• A button to reset all records and start fresh.

Truly private
Your records are stored only on your phone. No account or email needed, and the app works offline.

Features
• Real-time smoke-free timer.
• Motivational messages based on your progress.
• Today and all-time stats.
• Editable history.
• Clean, easy-to-use dark design.
• Free, with unobtrusive ads.

This app is a support tool and is not a substitute for advice from a healthcare professional. If you want help quitting, talk to your doctor or pharmacist.
